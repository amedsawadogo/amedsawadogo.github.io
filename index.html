🛡️ Using Headroom Safely

The safest way to use Headroom is to look first and act second. Begin with the treemap or the folder tree and take note of the areas that seem unusually large. Then open those areas and review the contents. Read the safety guidance shown in the app before you confirm any removal.

Some folders belong to the operating system or to applications you rely on. These should be left alone unless you fully understand them. Headroom's guidance is there to help you tell the difference between files you created and files that keep your Mac working.

If you are unsure about a folder, leave it in place. Freeing disk space is helpful, but a working Mac matters more. A careful, slow approach gives you the best results.

💡 Tips for a Cleaner Mac

Review your storage regularly rather than waiting until the disk is full. A quick monthly look through the treemap keeps you aware of growth before it becomes a problem.

Use categories to find patterns. Large amounts of the same file type often point to a folder you can reorganize, archive, or move to external storage.

Empty your downloads folder from time to time. It is one of the most common places where space quietly disappears.

Keep a copy of anything important before you remove it. Headroom's safety guidance exists because careful cleanup is better than fast regret.
